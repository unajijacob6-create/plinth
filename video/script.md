# Plinth demo video (version 2): narration and sources

Voiceover text, exactly as sent to ElevenLabs, is in `narration.txt` (one generation; 192 words; 66.5 s of
speech). The film places it in nine pieces (`SEGS` in `src/timeline.ts`) so each part has room on screen;
total length 83.5 s. Times below are video seconds. Footage is the live site
(https://unajijacob6-create.github.io/plinth/), recorded by `tools/record.cjs` on 2026-11-12 against
NEAR testnet, with no wallet connected: the launch form is shown up to Review and nothing is sent.
