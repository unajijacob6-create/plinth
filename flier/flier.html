Plinth

My submission for NEAR HACKATHON 2026

A shared fund nobody can change.

People pool tokens. The rules are fixed when the fund is created, and nobody holds a key. A manager can only suggest trades the rules allow, and anyone can take their share out at any time.

  • No keys

    Nobody can change it, not even its creator.

  • The manager only suggests

    The contract refuses anything outside the rules. A hijacked manager was refused on chain.

  • Leave any time

    Your slice of every token, no permission needed.
