Why it matters

Most funds ask you to trust a person. Plinth trusts the rules.

In most investment pools, someone holds a key that can change the rules, pause the fund or stop withdrawals. You trust that person not to. A Plinth fund has no key at all. Its rules are written once, when it is created, and nobody can change them afterwards, not even the people who built it.

How it works

Four steps. No one in charge.

A fund is a small program on NEAR, a public blockchain anyone can check. Here is its whole life.

  1. Step 1

    Create a fund with its rules

    Which tokens it may hold, how big a trade may be, how much a trade may cost. The fund is born with no key, so nobody can change it.

  2. Step 2

    People join by putting tokens in

    Each person gets shares for what they add. A share is a claim on a slice of everything the fund holds.

  3. Step 3

    A manager can only propose

    A person, an AI, or no one at all can suggest a trade. The contract checks it against the rules and refuses anything else.

  4. Step 4

    Anyone leaves with their slice

    At any time, without asking anyone, you take your share of every token the fund holds. No price feed, no manager.

Questions

In plain words

Is this real money?

No. Plinth runs on NEAR testnet, a public network for testing. The tokens are test tokens that anyone can get for free, and they are worth nothing.

What is NEAR, and what is a contract?

NEAR is a public blockchain: a shared computer whose records anyone can read. A contract is a small program that lives on it. Each Plinth fund is one contract with its own account.

Who can change a fund?

Nobody. A fund's account has no keys, so no one can sign for it: not to change its code, not to change its rules, not to take money out. The verifier checks this from the chain, in your browser.

What if the manager is hacked?

The manager can only suggest trades. The contract checks each one against the fund's rules and refuses the rest. A hacked manager can do no more than an honest one: propose a trade inside the rules.

Can I always leave?

Yes. You can take your share of every token the fund holds at any time, without asking anyone. If a trade is in flight, you wait up to 10 minutes.

Is it safe to put real money in?

Not yet. Plinth has only run on testnet. A contract with no keys cannot be fixed if a bug is found, and one already was, in our earlier releases. The Security page explains it.
